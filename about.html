01 / About

Engineer first, researcher always.

Seven years between computer-vision labs and production engineering — and the same instinct in both: take the hard part yourself, then make it fast.

I started in computer-vision labs — training drowsiness detectors and retinal screening models — and never really left that mindset.

Today I ship consumer software at Kahf: a privacy-first browser, a content-safety extension, an audio filter, and a kids' video platform. All four live or die on latency and trust, which is a good discipline. When SafeGaze had to run on low-end laptops, the answer wasn't a bigger model — it was rebuilding the execution path until it was three times faster.

Before that I spent nearly five years on Rakizo, a no-code app builder, from an empty repository to a live multi-tenant platform on Google Cloud: microservices, event-driven operations, a code-generation service backed by a custom LLM, and the CI/CD to keep all of it honest. I led the local team and wrote most of the services myself.

I care about the unglamorous parts — the caching layer, the dead-letter queue, the model small enough to run on a device. And I like handing that knowledge on: since 2024 I've been teaching Python to bootcamp cohorts at HDNB.
